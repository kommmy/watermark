## Description

markit is a local photo watermark and collage tool for iPhone. Pick a photo and markit reads available EXIF details such as camera model, lens, aperture, shutter speed, ISO, date, and location text, then turns them into a clean photo card.

Switch between multiple visual styles, from minimal white borders and monochrome cards to film strips, magazine covers, and journal-inspired layouts. It is built for photographers, travel notes, daily snapshots, and anyone who wants a photo to feel more finished before sharing.

markit also includes collage layouts for combining several images into one polished cover. Choose common ratios such as 3:4, 1:1, 4:5, and 9:16, then save the result to Photos or share it through the system share sheet.

Key features:

- Read EXIF details from selected photos.
- Generate camera-style photo watermarks.
- Switch between multiple photo card templates.
- Create camera collages and multi-photo layouts.
- Edit missing or private metadata before exporting.
- Process photos locally, with no account required.
- Save to Photos or share through iOS.
